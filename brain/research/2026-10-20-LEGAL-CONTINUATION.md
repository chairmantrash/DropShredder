## Implemented audit correction

The metadata helper previously checked record-level expiry and presence of a successful-source field, allowing a newly dated summary to mask an older source. It now checks each source's actual successful retrieval date, date validity, attempt chronology and retrieval outcome. The oldest source can expire the observation; a failed response carrying an old success string cannot authenticate a body. Ledger validation also rejects record expiry extending beyond any retrieved source's seven-day limit.

Additional checks stop proposed orders, redress updates, closed inquiries, procedural withdrawal and scheduled hearings from being encoded as established adverse merits adjudications. Dataset publication cutoffs cannot silently become future access dates. Six additional regressions cover these gaps, supplement continuity and exact proceeding scope. These checks apply to authored research metadata; no production importer, standing calculation, user permission or backend was changed.
