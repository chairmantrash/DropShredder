## Standing tools and what their ratings mean

BBB letter grades, customer stars, accreditation and complaint resolution are distinct. Customer reviews do not set the letter grade; non-accreditation/NR/no profile is neutral. Fees for accreditation must not become a quality penalty. A current A/B-range bureau record plus current separately published active-entity evidence can yield favorable evidence in the reviewed sources, not a universal trust guarantee. BBB D/F or exact official final adverse/restriction records can produce adverse standing context; allegations/warnings remain unresolved. Positive evidence cannot erase adverse evidence: the result is mixed. Complaint counts alone lack sales-volume and resolution context.

GLEIF, SEC and company registries help legal identity. LEI LAPSED concerns registration renewal; it is not necessarily corporate inactivity. Corporations Canada covers federal corporations rather than every provincial company. DENUE is establishment discovery, not product quality certification. Exact legal name, entity domain, listing and role are user checked; similar names/shared hosting are insufficient.

PROFECO Buró, Distintivo Digital and online-store monitoring are valuable Mexican research references but have coverage/status/reuse limits. The Buró's reported coverage through August 31, 2026 is not a live universal company clearance. Pending/private identities and incomplete searches prevent no-hit conclusions. Online monitoring's commercial reuse restriction means these references are link-only; no scraped data or imported rating is bundled. Digital distinction/provider requirements do not establish every product's origin.

EcoVadis assesses scoped organizational sustainability; its medal is not a product certificate or endorsement. Its current scope/validity and recognition page matter. Sedex membership and SMETA audits are not certification. amfori BSCI grades/site cycles/follow-up assess scoped social compliance and reports can require membership; they cannot be converted into overall product trust. B Corp's directory body was unreadable and Certipedia retrieval failed in this audit: both remain link-only with no current company certification implied. UL Product iQ, OEKO-TEX, GOTS and USB-IF checks need exact certificate/model/scope/status rather than a generic logo or company name.

CBP WRO uses a different evidentiary threshold from a Finding; modified/revoked/superseded actions must replace stale snapshots. OFAC/CSL search candidates are due-diligence leads, and replicated government feeds share a publisher family rather than multiplying independent evidence. USMCA origin certification concerns specific eligible goods/shipments and applicable rules, not a universal company compliance badge or a claim of 100% regional raw inputs. Trade and origin dimensions remain separate.
