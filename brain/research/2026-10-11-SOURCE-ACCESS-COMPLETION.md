## Current limits confirmed

BBB grades, reviews, accreditation and complaint resolution remain distinct. Complaint counts need transaction and resolution context. EcoVadis recognition applies to the assessed entity for twelve months; it is not product certification and requires recognition-page context. Sedex questionnaires/member audits do not form a free public score feed. GLEIF LAPSED concerns renewal, not necessarily inactivity. Corporations Canada's federal database excludes provincial/territorial and certain financial corporations.

Fashion Nova's Pending header coexists with an older final-order timeline: procedural documents and dates outrank summary headers. Competition Bureau entries distinguish investigations, applications and registered agreements. PACER free opinions require registration and do not replace current dockets. Canada's Federal Court index may lag, is not the official court record, and can require bilingual party queries. FMCSA snapshots concern carrier identity/safety, not litigation or seller trust.
