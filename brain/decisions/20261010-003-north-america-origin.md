# DS-041: regional product origin is a separate, user-reviewed coverage assessment
Date: 2026-10-10
Status: accepted implementation decision under current owner directive

Owner requested Canada/Mexico dropshipping research, an outlet/source map and a utility for products entirely made, sold and shipped within North America. Existing evidence policies and $0/local-first constraints remain authoritative. DS-040 Hindi OCR correctness is unchanged and remains blocked.

Define this utility's operational region as US/CA/MX and show that scope everywhere. Require eight independent role coverages: raw materials, components/ingredients, processing, assembly, packaging, seller of record, first physical dispatch and sale destination. Company addresses, flags, platform/CDN signatures, shipping speed, barcode prefixes, preferential trade certificates and statutory origin labels are insufficient. Country/geography/provider membership remain unscored.

Build claim-only origin assessment into explicit full scans. Local 50 KB dossiers are read only by shopper action, preview sources with safe links, require deliberate user review, match exact listing/seller/GTIN or brand/model/variant/specifications, and expire conservatively. JSON schema/source-type/coverage fields are self-authored and cannot authenticate documentary truth. Positive copy is DOCUMENTED (user-reviewed sources), never VERIFIED/CERTIFIED. No real product starts with positive status; source/merchant directory membership does not grant it. Incomplete or contradictory evidence abstains.

Dossiers stay in panel memory, clear on navigation, do not trigger source retrieval, and do not update old local history. Applying changes only the current report and safe export. Initial claim-only assessments may be in normal scan history; no full dossiers, private documents, shipping addresses, purchaser identity or raw document detail enter it. Source links cannot contain query tokens. No backend, new permission, dependencies or automatic remote research.

Record dated primary-source provider/merchant facts as neutral leads with limits. PROFECO results have commercial reuse restrictions: link to public consumer tools rather than importing scores or selling trust badges. Government, trade and manufacturer directories establish different kinds of evidence; keep them separate. Native browser and representative live regional/SKU acceptance remain distinct from pure/DOM tests.
