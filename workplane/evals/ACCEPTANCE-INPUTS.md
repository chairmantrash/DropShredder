## Numeric proposal, awaiting the owner's decision

The separate proposal recommends at least 200 observations per checked stratum/cell, 95% lower confidence bounds of 0.95 warning precision and 0.80 recall, a 95% upper false-positive bound of 0.02 and upper UNKNOWN bound of 0.80. These are reviewable recommendations, not approved launch thresholds or measured accuracy. `corpus-template.json` retains `thresholds:null` and no approval.

With zero observed errors, the proposed Wilson bounds still need at least 73 true warnings and 189 independently adjudicated benign cases in each checked group. These denominator floors do not provide the corpus or satisfy the other coverage/recall/UNKNOWN gates. Group-dependent observations cannot be represented as independent samples without review. No universal factorial coverage or calibrated fraud probability is claimed.

## Avoid calibration leakage

The built-in source/merchant registries, fixture tests and named case studies were available during development. For example, HaremPants/Sure Design and the Geeksoutfit-like family are existing development controls. Review all relevant source and test exposures, canonicalize shared operators/product variants, and populate `calibrationGroups` before evaluation. Empty calibration arrays are not proof of independence. Source platform and merchant-group identity are different: marketplace sellers need actual operator grouping.

## Photograph-source research

Open Food Facts' official documentation describes contributor-taken packaging pictures and CC Attribution ShareAlike image licensing, while warning that graphical elements can carry separate rights. Its database has separate ODbL/DbCL obligations and no accuracy assurance. This is a possible source for a reviewer to assess, not an approved merchant corpus or blanket rights clearance. Food-only photographs do not satisfy all requested commerce categories. No images or labels were imported and no native/legal approval was inferred.
