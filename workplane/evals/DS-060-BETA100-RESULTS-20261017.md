The160-to158 difference is retained, with changed site availability, page state, privacy refusals and unconfirmed commerce recorded separately. Blocked/refused pages and numerical abstentions do not count as successful detections. Zero severe warnings is not evidence of a zero false-positive rate or high recall.

The Ordinary and Round Lab previously received strong shipping-conflict findings from temporal/marketing prose. Both now scan without that finding. Shipment parsing requires explicit wording, separates origin from destination, respects negation/quotes and compares only unambiguous recognized jurisdictions. Genuine shipment-origin controls still pass.

The review extractor now connects visible current-product Judge.me reviews to existing review analysis. Product URLs, variants, hidden/private regions, duplicate renderings and widget scope are checked. Live review analyses were observed on Pact, Finex and Warmly; a separate owned seven-review native control verifies duplication analysis while excluding hidden/unrelated/store-wide copies. Missing verification state remains unknown. Unsupported widget shapes and unrendered reviews remain outside coverage.

Context notes no longer produce alarming on-page stamps. All126 comparable context-only retest scans show no warning, and the native control verifies NO VERDICT in Professional, Aggressive and Nuclear modes. Severe evidence gates and score thresholds remain unchanged.
