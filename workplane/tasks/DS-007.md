# DS-007 — Local observation history
Status: RUNNING
Owner: runtime

## Goal
Persist product/report observations locally for chronology, fake scarcity, supplier drift, and trend-wave analysis.

## Constraints
- IndexedDB/local only.
- No telemetry/cloud sync.
- Identity prefers GTIN, then brand+MPN, then canonical URL, then domain+title fallback.
- Persistence failure must not prevent a scan verdict.

## Acceptance
- A completed scan can be stored.
- Prior observations can be retrieved by product identity.
- Timestamp/price/report are retained.
