# DS-021 — Weekly dropshipper enablement intelligence watch
Status: RUNNING
Owner: research

## Goal
Continuously track public tools, apps, platforms and training material that help dropshippers source products, disguise provenance, automate fulfillment, import/syndicate reviews, generate product pages/ads, manufacture scarcity, brand tracking, private-label products, and research competitors.

## Weekly scan families
- sourcing / fulfillment automation
- product/ad research and competitor-spy tools
- AI store/product-page builders
- review importers/syndication
- private-label/custom-packaging services
- branded tracking and fulfillment UX
- scarcity/FOMO/conversion apps
- image/background/watermark/copy transformation
- seller training that explicitly recommends new operational techniques

## Output
For meaningful changes:
1. update `brain/research/` dated intelligence note;
2. update `intelligence/tooling-registry.json`;
3. add candidate rules only when an observable artifact exists;
4. document false-positive risk;
5. create or update regression fixture/task for any rule promoted to strong/direct;
6. never negatively score nationality, country of manufacture, Shopify, CDN, payment processor, or tool use alone.

## Principle
We are not trying to identify people because they use a particular legitimate ecommerce tool. We are learning which old detection heuristics become obsolete and which durable, independently verifiable traces remain.
