## Goal
Offer useful, conservative verdicts on actual product listing pages after explicit site-access consent, without bothering people during unrelated browsing, store categories, search results, account, checkout or payment pages.

## Scope
- Research primary Chrome APIs and Google merchant/product structured-data distinctions.
- Lightweight bounded page classification, multiple independent page signals, fail closed if uncertain.
- Dynamically registered ISOLATED content script after user opts in to automatic HTTPS site access. No permanent host permissions / no tabs permission.
- Short evidence-based toast with user-click side-panel handoff for explicit deeper scan.
- SPA-aware bounded behavior; no continuous heavy scanning.
- Settings/permission lifecycle and regressions; artifact and Store policy review.

## Important
Users who do not grant optional site access retain the manual scan path. Only product pages trigger automatic toasts. Account/checkout/payment never scanned. Severe labels require independent evidence. Do not merge PR #9.
