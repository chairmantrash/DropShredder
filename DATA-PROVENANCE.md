## Optional external material

GLEIF's LEI/reference data Access Service specifies CC0 and free access. Use its offered API, attribute the source descriptively, do not copy logos or imply endorsement. Current runtime retrieves one user-supplied exact LEI and does not bundle the global database.

RDAP responses have registry-specific terms and potential personal fields; the bootstrap CC0 license does not license every registry response. Current controls retrieve bounded selected chronology with a short session cache, not a redistribution dataset.

CPSC's public candidate query is informational and may return unrelated substring hits; no full database or third-party imagery is redistributed. Brave response use is subject to the user's provider plan/terms; no cached shared results database or sublicensing claim is made. Review sites/search/image services are explicit destinations, not imported review/rating/image databases.

User-supplied signed or unsigned reference lists must carry source/license metadata. Signature validity authenticates bytes against the pinned key, not issuer identity, copyright ownership or product truth. The extension cannot verify whether a publisher owns everything it declares. A publisher must review each source's scope/rights before redistributing records or images. No official shared feed publisher, signing private key or hosted backend is installed by this release packet.

## Research and restricted candidates

Research JSON/Markdown, competitor references, model/API candidates, complaint links and short summaries remain engineering context. They are not wholesale imported into the Chrome package; WXT imports the RDAP JSON and runtime TS tables, and copies public assets. A project catalogue is not permission to redistribute a source's dataset.

Do not promote a candidate with unresolved licenses/terms into a runtime corpus. Keep it research-only until code/data/model licenses, dependencies, provider conditions, privacy and scope are separately recorded. This includes proprietary spy-tool indices, commercial reviews, reverse-image result photos and model weights. Current review does not authorize scraping/login/CAPTCHA bypass.
